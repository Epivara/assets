Intelligence

Near the message box you'll see an intelligence selector. It's a simple menu that decides, behind the scenes, which model answers you and how much it “thinks” before replying — you don't have to know the model names to use it.

What it actually controls

Placeholder: higher intelligence means a stronger model and more deliberate thinking — better for hard problems, slower and more expensive for simple ones. Lower is quick and light for everyday questions.

You usually don't have to touch it

Placeholder: by default ChatGPT reads your prompt and bumps itself up to higher intelligence when it decides the task warrants it, so leaving it on the default is fine for most work. Reach for the selector when you want to force more thinking on a tough problem, or keep it light to save time.

Ties into Usage More intelligence costs more under the hood. If you're working against the weekly limit, this is one of the dials that affects how fast you spend — see Usage.
