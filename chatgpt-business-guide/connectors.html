Connectors

Connectors let ChatGPT reach into the other tools you already use — Gmail, Dropbox, Zoom, Canva, QuickBooks — so it can pull in real information instead of guessing.

Important Connectors are per-user and self-serve. There is no admin switch that turns them on for everyone — you have to enable each connector on your own account. This is the single most common thing new hires miss.
