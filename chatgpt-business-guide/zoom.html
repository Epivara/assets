Zoom

The Zoom plugin lets ChatGPT pull in what Zoom has already saved from your meetings — transcripts and AI Companion summaries.

Retrieve-only — Zoom has to capture it first The plugin only retrieves what Zoom already recorded; it never joins or listens to a live meeting. If Zoom isn't saving transcripts or AI Companion summaries, there's nothing for ChatGPT to fetch.
