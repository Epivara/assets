Work

Work is the mode for heavier, longer-running jobs — the kind that take many steps and lean on a lot of source material. Where Chat is for quick, back-and-forth tasks, Work is where you'd hand off something like “go through these twelve spreadsheets and pull the numbers into one summary” and let it grind.

Heads-up on limits Work draws on a separate weekly usage limit that it shares with Codex — it's not unlimited the way Chat is. Before you lean on it heavily, read Usage so you know what you're spending.
