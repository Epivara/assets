Signing In

ChatGPT Business is accessed and managed at chatgpt.com, the same website normal users use.

To access ChatGPT Business, you first need an invite. Once you're in, you select the business workspace rather than your personal account.

1. Get your invite

Access starts with an invite from our admin. You will receive an invite to the inbox of the email address being invited. Once you've accepted, you should now have access to our ChatGPT Business workspace.

2. Sign in at chatgpt.com

Go to chatgpt.com and sign in with the same email address the invite was sent to.

If you already use ChatGPT with a personal account, you don't have to sign out; web ChatGPT can hold more than one account.

3. Select the Epivara workspace

To start using ChatGPT Business, make sure your Business workspace account is selected.

Open the bottom-left corner menu and choose the Epivara workspace.

Select the Epivara workspace.

Watch out Anything you do in the personal account tied to your email stays personal. It is separate from Epivara's workspace. Make sure you have the Epivara workspace account selected to access Business features.

Want an app instead of a browser tab?

This guide mostly covers ChatGPT in your web browser. There's also a desktop app if you want ChatGPT with access to local files. Using the app is a bit more involved, so it has its own page: see Desktop App.
