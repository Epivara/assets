Sites

Sites lets you build a web page by describing it to ChatGPT. It builds the site and hosts it on the Internet for you.

Creating a site

In the desktop app, open the Sites page from the sidebar and press Create. Or, start one from any chat by @ tagging Sites.

Describe the site you want and ChatGPT will build it. From there, you can make additional requests to change the way the site looks.

Sharing your site

Your finished sites live on the Sites page. To let others see a site, press Share and choose the audience. They will need the link to visit the site.

Still rolling out Sites is easy to find in the desktop app, but at the moment, the feature is hidden on the web. If you want to manage your sites on the web, navigate directly to chatgpt.com/sites.
