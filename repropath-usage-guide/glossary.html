Glossary

This glossary provides details about each metric provided by the modules offered. Pixel measurements are converted to physical units using each image's um_per_px scale.

Tubule Module

Tubules are first classified into intact tubules, damaged tubules (not intact), and cross-sections. Cross sections are determined as intact tubules that are circular, do not touch the image edge, and — for adult samples — contain a visible lumen.

Several metrics are computed only on cross-sections for consistency and accuracy.

  • count
    • The number of tubules objects detected in the image. Fragments classified as "not intact" are excluded; both ordinary tubules and cross-sections are counted.
  • tubule_percent
    • The percentage of the image's total area that is occupied by tubules. Computed as total tubule pixel area divided by the whole image area. Fragments classified as "not intact" are excluded.
  • diameter
    • The length (µm) of the minor axis of the ellipse best fitted to the tubule (the ellipse with the same normalized second central moments as the tubule region — see scikit-image regionprops axis_minor_length). The minor axis is used because it is the more stable estimate of true tubule width on a slightly oblique cut. Only computed on tubule cross-sections.
  • lumen_percent
    • The average percentage of a tubule's area that is taken up by its lumen. For each tubule, computed as lumen pixels divided by the tubule's total pixels. If the detected lumen is smaller than 3% of the tubule area it is treated as no lumen (0%), to avoid counting small bright specks as a lumen. Only computed across tubule cross-sections.
  • epithelial_thickness
    • The thickness (µm) of the germ-cell (epithelial) layer. Computed by taking each point along the lumen edge and measuring its straight-line distance to the nearest point on the outer tubule boundary, then taking the median of those per-point distances. (The median is used so a jagged lumen boundary does not skew the result.) Only computed on tubule cross-sections that have exactly one connected lumen region.

Leydig Module

Leydig metrics are reported for three groups: Round Leydig cells, Spindle Leydig cells, and All Leydig cells (round + spindle combined). Nuclei are detected and classified by a nuclear model; Leydig cell cytoplasm for round cells are detected separately by a whole-cell model. A round nucleus is matched to a cell body by checking which cell body its center falls inside.

For round Leydig cells, this glossary will refer to the cytoplasm as "whole cells" and the nuclei as nuclei.

Round Leydig metrics:

  • round_leydig_cell_area
    • The area (µm²) of each whole cell. A whole cell is counted whether or not a nucleus was matched to it — this metric includes cells without a visible nucleus. Conversely, a round Leydig nucleus that has no visible cytoplasm (no whole cell) is not considered in this metric.
  • round_leydig_nuclear_area
    • The area (µm²) of a round Leydig cell nucleus.
  • round_leydig_cytoplasm_area
    • The area (µm²) of the cytoplasm of a round Leydig cell, computed as the cell-body area minus its matched nucleus area (clamped to ≥ 0). Only computed for round nuclei that were successfully matched to a cell body.
  • round_leydig_nuclear_cytoplasmic_ratio
    • The ratio of a round Leydig nucleus's area to its matched whole cell area. I.e. the fraction of the entire cell occupied by the nucleus. Note that despite the name, the denominator is the whole cell area, not the cytoplasm-only area. Only computed for matched round nucleus to whole cell pairs.
  • round_leydig_nuclear_eccentricity
    • The eccentricity of each round Leydig nucleus, measuring how elongated the nucleus is. From 0 (perfect circle) to 1 (long, thin ellipse). Taken from the best-fit ellipse (scikit-image regionprops eccentricity).
  • round_leydig_cell_count
    • The number of round whole cell instances detected.
  • round_leydig_nuclei_count
    • The number of round Leydig cell nuclei detected.
  • round_leydig_nuclei_area_percent_patch
    • The percentage of the whole image area covered by round Leydig nuclei (total round nucleus pixels divided by total image pixels).
  • round_leydig_nuclei_area_percent_interstitium
    • The percentage of the interstitium (image area minus tubule area) covered by round Leydig nuclei.
  • round_leydig_cell_area_percent_patch
    • The percentage of the whole image area covered by whole cells (total whole-cell pixels divided by total image pixels).
  • round_leydig_cell_area_percent_interstitium
    • The percentage of the interstitium (image area minus tubule area) covered by whole cells.

Spindle Leydig metrics:

Spindle Leydig cells have essentially no visible cytoplasm to segment, so only their nuclei are measured.

  • spindle_leydig_nuclear_area
    • The area (µm²) of a spindle Leydig cell nucleus.
  • spindle_leydig_nuclear_eccentricity
    • The eccentricity of each spindle Leydig nucleus — how elongated it is, from 0 (a circle) to 1 (a long, thin ellipse).
  • spindle_leydig_nuclei_count
    • The number of spindle Leydig cell nuclei detected.
  • spindle_leydig_nuclei_area_percent_patch
    • The percentage of the whole image area covered by spindle Leydig nuclei.
  • spindle_leydig_nuclei_area_percent_interstitium
    • The percentage of the interstitium (image area minus tubule area) covered by spindle Leydig nuclei.

All Leydig metrics:

  • all_leydig_nuclear_area
    • The area (µm²) of a Leydig cell nucleus, round and spindle nuclei combined.
  • all_leydig_cell_area
    • The area (µm²) of a Leydig cell, round and spindle combined. For round Leydig cells the whole cell area is used. For spindle Leydig cells the nuclear area is used.
  • all_leydig_round_spindle_ratio
    • The ratio of the number of round Leydig nuclei to the number of spindle Leydig nuclei. Reported as empty when no spindle nuclei are present (division by zero).
  • all_leydig_nuclei_area_percent_patch
    • The percentage of the whole image area covered by all Leydig nuclei. Round + spindle nucleus pixels divided by total image pixels.
  • all_leydig_nuclei_area_percent_interstitium
    • The percentage of the interstitium (image area minus tubule area) covered by all Leydig nuclei (round + spindle).
  • all_leydig_cell_area_percent_patch
    • The percentage of the whole image area covered by all Leydig cells. Uses the whole cells for round Leydig cells plus spindle nucleus pixels, divided by total image pixels.
  • all_leydig_cell_area_percent_interstitium
    • The percentage (%) of the interstitium (image area minus tubule area) covered by all Leydig cells, using round whole cell pixels plus spindle nucleus pixels.

Tubule context (Leydig)

The Leydig module runs the tubule model for internal purposes. Metrics are reported for full disclosure. These are computed the same way as the Tubule module metrics.

Spermatogenesis Module

The spermatogenesis module detects and classifies germ cells only inside tubule cross-sections.

For every cell type there are two metrics: a total count across the image (ROI), and a count per tubule list (one number per cross-section in the image). For the count per tubule list, the indices are shared across metrics, e.g. the first entries of sertoli_count_per_tubule and spermatogonia_count_per_tubule belong to the same tubule.

  • sertoli_count
    • The total number of Sertoli cells detected across all tubule cross-sections in the image.
  • gonocyte_count
    • The total number of gonocytes detected across all tubule cross-sections in the image.
  • spermatogonia_count
    • The total number of spermatogonia detected across all tubule cross-sections in the image.
  • spermatocyte_count
    • The total number of spermatocytes detected across all tubule cross-sections in the image.
  • round_spermatid_count
    • The total number of round spermatids detected across all tubule cross-sections in the image.
  • elongated_spermatid_count
    • The total number of elongated spermatids detected across all tubule cross-sections in the image.
  • sertoli_count_per_tubule
    • A list of per-tubule Sertoli cell counts — one entry per tubule cross-section, giving the number of Sertoli cells found inside that individual tubule.
  • gonocyte_count_per_tubule
    • A list of per-tubule gonocyte counts — one entry per tubule cross-section, giving the number of gonocytes found inside that individual tubule.
  • spermatogonia_count_per_tubule
    • A list of per-tubule spermatogonia counts — one entry per tubule cross-section, giving the number of spermatogonia found inside that individual tubule.
  • spermatocyte_count_per_tubule
    • A list of per-tubule spermatocyte counts — one entry per tubule cross-section, giving the number of spermatocytes found inside that individual tubule.
  • round_spermatid_count_per_tubule
    • A list of per-tubule round spermatid counts — one entry per tubule cross-section, giving the number of round spermatids found inside that individual tubule.
  • elongated_spermatid_count_per_tubule
    • A list of per-tubule elongated spermatid counts — one entry per tubule cross-section, giving the number of elongated spermatids found inside that individual tubule.
  • tubule_count
    • The number of tubule cross-sections analyzed in the image (the tubules within which germ cells were counted).
